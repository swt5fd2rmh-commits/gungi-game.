Rules & controls

Choose a piece from your hand and tap a highlighted square to deploy. Tap one of your own towers to select its top piece, then tap a highlighted destination. Tap the selected tower again to deselect. Towers can contain up to two or three pieces depending on ruleset; only the top piece moves. You can move onto friendly towers to stack, or onto an enemy tower to capture all enemy pieces (preserving friendly pieces), or to stack instead. Captured pieces leave the game; there is no captured-piece hand.

During setup, alternate placing pieces in your own three rows. Kings must be placed before setup can end. Stacking up to the ruleset limit is allowed during setup; no two friendly Pawns in the same column during setup. Select “Finish setup” after both Marshals are down, or use Quick start for a full preset deployment. During play, Arata drops can reach as far as your most advanced friendly piece. Capturing a King wins.

Quick Start uses a balanced full-roster house preset (not the rulebook’s illustrated configuration). Custom piece names and symbols are shown in each reserve. All 14 rulebook piece types are included (with their rulebook names). Movement grows with tower tier. Blue advances down; Red advances up. Movement is adapted from the supplied rulebook diagrams. Tier 2 and Tier 3 add to the lower-tier moves. Optional Captain escort and Cannon vault are house rules, disabled by default; the photographed movement charts do not establish those abilities. Movement diagrams still require exact rulebook comparison. Check/checkmate is not implemented. Fourfold repetition of the same complete position is a draw. Enemy towers of equal or lower height can be captured; enemy pieces are removed while friendly pieces remain. Long non-jumping moves cannot pass through occupied squares.

Special abilities and AI

Optional house-rule Captain escort (off by default): when a Captain is on top of a friendly stack, moving it may carry the friendly piece immediately underneath, provided both fit at the destination and the ruleset permits the stack. Enable or disable the escort using the checkbox below.

Optional house-rule Cannon vault (off by default): Cannons at tier 2+ may vault over exactly one occupied square in a straight line, landing on the square immediately beyond it; landing follows standard stack and capture restrictions.

AI levels: Easy chooses randomly; Medium evaluates material and position; Hard and Expert look ahead at opposing responses with increasingly broad searches. AI controls Red in Computer AI mode, or both colors in AI vs AI mode. AI decisions are heuristic, not guaranteed optimal.

Rule verification

Verified from supplied rulebook page 5: Cannon, Musketeer, and Archer can jump over friendly or enemy towers only if the crossed tower is the same height or lower than the jumping piece. Tier 2 and 3 retain earlier movement options.

Still provisional: points after two passes, and optional Captain Escort/Cannon Vault. These are not established by the supplied movement pages.

Move history

Points house rule: all remaining non-King pieces on the board and in reserves count toward each color’s score. Fourfold repetition of the same complete position (including side to move and reserves) ends in a draw. Two consecutive passes during play end the game; higher score wins, equal scores draw. Capturing the King still wins immediately.

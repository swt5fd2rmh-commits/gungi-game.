Rules & controls

Choose a piece from your hand and tap a highlighted square to deploy. Tap one of your own towers to select its top piece, then tap a highlighted destination. Tap the selected tower again to deselect. Towers can contain up to three pieces; only the top piece moves. You can move onto friendly towers to stack, or onto an enemy tower to capture its top piece. Captured pieces leave the game; there is no captured-piece hand.

During setup, alternate placing pieces in your own three rows. Marshals must be placed before setup can end. No stacking during setup and no two friendly Pawns in the same column during setup. Select “Finish setup” after both Marshals are down, or use Quick start. During play, remaining hand pieces can be dropped only into your territory. Capturing a Marshal wins.

Piece abbreviations: M Marshal, G General, L Lieutenant, J Major, P Pawn, S Spy, C Cannon, F Fortress, A Samurai, T Captain. Movement grows with tower tier. Red advances down; Blue advances up. The movement profiles below are a playable house-rules interpretation, not a verified official anime ruleset. This edition includes optional Captain escort and Cannon vault abilities, AI, and position scoring. The movement profiles and special abilities are a configurable fan interpretation, not verified canonical Hunter × Hunter rules. Check/checkmate and repetition adjudication are not implemented.

Special abilities and AI

Captain escort: when a Captain is on top of a friendly stack, moving it may carry the piece immediately underneath as a unit, if the destination has room. Enable or disable the escort using the checkbox below.

Cannon vault: Cannons at tier 2+ may vault over exactly one occupied square in a straight line, landing on the square immediately beyond it; landing follows standard stack and capture restrictions.

AI levels: Easy chooses randomly; Medium evaluates material and position; Hard and Expert look ahead at opposing responses with increasingly broad searches. AI controls Blue. AI decisions are heuristic, not guaranteed optimal.
